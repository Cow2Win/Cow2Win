What is the CowScore?

The CowScore rates how well a team fits one particular fortification.
So the same team can have a different CowScore at every fortification.
Cow2Win uses it to distribute the teams over the fortifications
and to leave the weakest teams out.

How is it made up?

CowScore = team power ÷ ${powerDivisor} × (1 + bonus)

The team power is the most important part. The bonus mainly decides between teams of similar power.

Bonus for hero teams

  • Role buff: +${rolePercent} % for every hero whose role matches the fortification's buff.
  • Relation: +${relationPercent} % once if at least one hero is marked "Positive" for this fortification, -${relationPercent} % if one is marked "Negative". Both together cancel each other out.
  • Pet: +${petPercent} % if the pet is marked for this fortification. Without a mark it only counts through its power.
  • War flag: +${warFlagPercent} % if it is marked for this fortification, otherwise +${warFlagPresentPercent} % just for having one.
  • Combo: +${comboPercent} % once if an active hero combo is complete in the team.

Bonus for titan teams

  • Element buff: +${elementPercent} % for every titan whose element matches the fortification's buff.
  • Relation: as for heroes, ±${relationPercent} % once per team.
  • Totems: +${totemPercent} % per totem (at most two).

Whoever already counts through the buff does not count again through their mark.
You set the percentages under Settings → CowScore bonuses.

Example

A hero team with ${examplePower} power defends the Foundry (buff: Tank).

With war flag and "Positive" mark

  • 2 tanks in the team: 2 × ${rolePercent} % = ${exampleRole} %
  • another hero is marked "Positive" for the Foundry: +${relationPercent} %
  • a war flag is in the team, but not marked: +${warFlagPresentPercent} %

Bonus: ${exampleBonus} %

CowScore = ${examplePower} ÷ ${powerDivisor}
× ${exampleFactor} ≈ ${exampleScore}

Same power, without war flag and without "Positive" mark

  • 2 tanks in the team: 2 × ${rolePercent} % = ${example2Role} %
  • no hero marked: +0 %
  • no war flag: +0 %

Bonus: ${example2Bonus} %

CowScore = ${examplePower} ÷ ${powerDivisor}
× ${example2Factor} ≈ ${example2Score}
